# EX-0113 — Draw a ternary relationship with an attribute

**Time:** ~22 min · **Skill:** `DB-ERM-NARY`

**Task:** Suppliers (supplier_id) agree to deliver Components (component_id) to Factories (factory_id). Each triple has at most one unit_price; any participant can have zero agreements. Draw a Chen ternary relationship.

**Checks:** One diamond, three participants, and unit_price on the relationship.
