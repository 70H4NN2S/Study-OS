# EX-0101 — Separate entities, attributes and actions

**Time:** ~8 min · **Skill:** `DB-ERM-SCOPE`

**Task:** A neighborhood workshop records members (member number, nickname), storage cabinets (cabinet number, size), and who rents which cabinet. Members can reset their password through the website. Identify entity types, their attributes, a persistent relationship, and an application action.

**Checks:** Do not model the password-reset operation as an ER relationship. Explain one choice.
